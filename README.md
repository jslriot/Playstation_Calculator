Tutorial
Go to account.playstation.com and log into your PSN account. From there, go to Account Management (you'll usually find it under your profile settings), then click on Transaction History.

By default, it'll show you the last two years of activity, but you can set a custom date range if you need to go back further. Once your transactions load, you'll see each one listed with a date, amount, and description that's exactly the format the calculator is looking for.

Just click and drag to highlight the transaction list, copy it (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C), and paste it straight into the Calculator.
