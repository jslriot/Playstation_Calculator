What we use

Strictly necessary storage: used to remember your cookie/privacy preference. It is not used for advertising or behavioural profiling.

Analytics: optional and disabled unless you actively choose it. No analytics provider is included in this build.

Your choices

You can choose “Strictly necessary only”, accept optional analytics, or customise your choice. You can change or withdraw your optional choice at any time using “Cookie settings”.

Consent records

When you make a choice, the site records a consent event containing a consent identifier, timestamp, consent/policy version, mechanism, selected categories and page path. This provides an auditable record of the choice and notice version.

We do not use the cookie mechanism to upload your PlayStation transaction history. The calculator processes transaction text in your browser.
